Founded in 2020 | 11 tonnes of recycled plastic in our Jakarta building | Collaborations with Nike, Goldwin & Dover Street Market

Circular design studio and cultural wellbeing community, turning plastic waste into objects, spaces and rituals.

It starts with waste. Space Available turns post-consumer plastic from Bali and Java into objects and spaces that brands like Nike, Goldwin and Dover Street Market put their name on. Around that core, our Self Care Community Centre in Kemang, Jakarta brings people together for meditation, sound, food and learning.
