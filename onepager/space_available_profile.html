OUR SPACE

The Self Care Community Centre (SCCC) by Space Available reimagines the role of physical retail as an ecosystem of cultural wellbeing. It is a hybrid space that brings together ritual practice, circular design and creative exchange, and it is crafted from over 11 tonnes of recycled plastic.

Brands and institutions come to Space Available to turn intention into something real: an object made from waste, a space people want to visit, a community that keeps coming back. We bring material, culture and community together under one roof.

  • 01Make sustainability tangibleObjects and spaces built from post-consumer plastic, not just claims.

  • 03Build community, not just reachWeekly rituals with a creative Jakarta audience.
  • 04Show the impactPlastic diverted, per object and per project.
