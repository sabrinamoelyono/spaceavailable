An Ecosystem of Cultural Wellbeing

Kemang, Jakarta

OUR SPACE

The Self Care Community Centre (SCCC) by Space Available reimagines the role of physical retail as an ecosystem of cultural wellbeing. It is a hybrid space that brings together ritual practice, circular design and creative exchange, and it is crafted from over 11 tonnes of recycled plastic.

The Practice

(how we work with others)

Each project starts with material that would otherwise be thrown away: post-consumer HDPE plastic collected in Bali and Java and made into objects in Indonesia. Between the studio and the Centre, our collaborations take many shapes, but they share one aim: shaping new models for how we live, gather and grow.

  • 01Circular objects & co-created capsules
  • 02Spaces, pop-ups & retail environments
  • 03Furniture & design editions
